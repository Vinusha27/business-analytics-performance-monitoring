# Business metrics

| KPI | Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Net revenue | completed item sales after discount | Realized sales value |
| AOV | net revenue / completed orders | Revenue per fulfilled order |
| Gross profit | net revenue − item cost | Profit before operating expenses |
| Gross margin | gross profit / net revenue | Unit-economics efficiency |
| Completion rate | completed orders / all orders | Fulfilment success |
| Return rate | returned orders / all orders | Post-sale quality or fit risk |
| Cancellation rate | cancelled orders / all orders | Pre-fulfilment operating risk |

Target status is **On Track** when the actual result reaches the configured target; rates with maximum thresholds use the inverse comparison.
